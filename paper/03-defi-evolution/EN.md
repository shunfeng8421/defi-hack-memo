# The Evolution of DeFi Attacks: A 10-Year Empirical Analysis of 824 Security Incidents

**Author**: Shiqiang Chen | **DOI**: 10.5281/zenodo.21382653

---

## Abstract

We present the largest empirical study of DeFi security incidents to date (824 attacks, 2017–2026). Risk index declined from 3.33% → 2.33% (↓30%). χ² test confirms attack patterns correlate with year (p < 0.001).

## Key Findings

| Finding | Value |
|------|:--:|
| Total cases | 824 |
| Time span | 2017–2026 |
| $\chi^2$ | 55.9 (p < 0.001) |
| Risk decline | 3.33% → 2.33% |
| Flash loan peak | 40.4% (2023) |

## Conclusion

The DeFi ecosystem is getting relatively safer despite more attacks. Flash loans remain dominant, reentrancy is declining, and AI agent contracts will be the next risk frontier.
