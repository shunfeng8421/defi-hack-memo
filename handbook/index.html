Why This Book Exists

"Security knowledge should be free. Top protocols spend millions on audits, but those who need it most — early-stage teams, independent developers, Web3 founders — have the least access."

Over five years, the author analyzed 824 DeFi exploit reports spanning from 2017 to 2026. One pattern emerged: most vulnerabilities are not novel. They are variations of known patterns — flash loan + spot oracle, cross-chain message replay, unprotected initializer functions — repeating with different protocol names and more victims each time.

This handbook classifies 66 attack patterns across 12 domains. Every pattern comes with a real case, real vulnerable code, a real fix, and a Foundry fork test that runs on mainnet state. You are not reading theory. You are reading crime scene reconstructions.
